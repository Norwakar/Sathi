# 🚶 Sathi — You Don't Have to Walk Alone

> *Sathi* means **friend** in Nepali.

## The Problem

In Kathmandu, thousands of teens commute daily — walking through Kamladi, 
Sahid Gate, Asan, Baneshwor — alone. When you walk with a friend, 
time disappears. When you walk alone, anxiety fills that space.

There's no app that solves this. So I'm building one.

## What Sathi Does

Sathi pairs teens heading in the **same direction** in real-time —
whether walking or on a public bus — so no one has to commute alone.
Two strangers become temporary companions for that journey.

## How AI Powers It

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Route Matching Agent | Finds peers with overlapping paths, not just same destinations |
| 🤝 Friend Score System | Builds trust over time using ratings, shared trips & behavior |
| 💬 Icebreaker AI | Suggests conversation starters based on shared interests |

## Why Kathmandu, Why Now

Kathmandu's teen commute culture is dense, chaotic, and deeply social —
yet no product has addressed the loneliness of doing it solo.
Sathi is built for this city first, with a vision to expand across Nepal.
